Badges are coming: things the app hands out when something worth celebrating happens. There are 150 ideas on the table, far too many on purpose. Help cut them down.

## Three kinds of badge

Each kind has its own look, so you can tell at a glance whose glory it is.

- **Yours** are for showing up, bringing people, pouring rounds, driving and taking a week off. They look like bottle caps: tin, brass, gold, and a holographic one for the rarest.
- **Your crew's** are for firsts, how big the crew gets, weekly streaks and the nights people remember. They look like beer mats: printed, then embossed, gold-foiled, framed.
- **A guild's** are for the huge numbers, and feats only many crews together can pull off. They look like supporters' club gear: scarves, enamel pins, real silverware.

## A taste

- **Standing Fixture** (crew): the same weekday six weeks running.
- **Total Football** (crew): a night where everyone at the table logged a beer for someone else.
- **The Chauffeur** (you): ten nights as the driver.
- **Steady Pace** (you, secret): ten nights in a row with three beers or fewer.
- **Matchday** (guild): ten crews out on the same day.
- **The Guild Million** (guild): one guild alone reaches 1.000.000.

One rule holds for all of them: no badge rewards drinking more in a single night. The table earns the glory, not the glass.
