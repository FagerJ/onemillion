One question decides how guilds work: who joins a guild — your whole crew, or each of you on your own? Below are four ways it could go, and what happens to the same night out in each.

## What a guild is

A guild is a banner many crews drink under, like a supporters' club. It gets its own milestones and badges, shown on the guild page and pushed to every member: the huge numbers (100.000 beers, one million) and feats only a guild can pull off, like 10 crews out on the same night. Your crew and the global million carry on exactly as now.

Whatever we pick, three things are already settled:

- A beer counts for **at most one** guild.
- Guilds are ranked on the badges they earn, not on how much they drink.
- Switching guild never rewrites the past: old beers stay with the guild they were drunk for.

## Four ways it could work

The question underneath them all: is a guild a club you support, or a league of friend groups?

### 1. Team

- **Who joins:** your whole crew joins one guild.
- **A beer counts:** always, every beer the crew logs.
- **Who decides:** the captain, once.
- **Good at:** it's simple. A guild is a league of crews, and crews compete as teams.
- **Weak spot:** a crew flies one flag. The AIK fans in a crew that joined Blåvitt drink for Blåvitt.

### 2. Supporter

- **Who joins:** each person backs one guild.
- **A beer counts:** when the drinker backs the guild **and** a fellow supporter is at the table.
- **Who decides:** each person, once.
- **Good at:** a guild is a club you back. One night can feed two clubs, a derby at the table.
- **Weak spot:** friends who don't follow football feed no guild, and a lone fan feeds nobody.

### 3. Matchday

- **Who joins:** nobody, for good. Each night picks a guild.
- **A beer counts:** when the night is declared for the guild and two or more of its members are there. Then every beer that night counts.
- **Who decides:** whoever starts the night, every night.
- **Good at:** it's flexible for crews with mixed loyalties.
- **Weak spot:** a choice, and an argument, every night out.

### 4. Hybrid

- **Who joins:** your crew joins a guild, and you can also back your own club.
- **A beer counts:** for your own club if a fellow supporter is at the table, otherwise for your crew's guild.
- **Who decides:** the captain once, plus anyone who wants their own club.
- **Good at:** every beer counts somewhere, and fans still back their own club.
- **Weak spot:** the most rules to explain. The crew's guild becomes a default people opt out of.

## Same night, four answers

A crew of six logs 20 beers on a Wednesday. Anna and Björn back Blåvitt (8 beers between them), Cecilia and David back AIK (6), and Elin and Oskar don't follow football (6). The crew as a whole joined Blåvitt, and under Matchday, Anna started the night and picked Blåvitt.

| Option | Beers for Blåvitt | Beers for AIK | Beers for no guild |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Team | 20 | 0 | 0 |
| 2. Supporter | 8 | 6 | 6 |
| 3. Matchday | 20 | 0 | 0 |
| 4. Hybrid | 14 | 6 | 0 |

If David had stayed home, Cecilia's beers would go nowhere under Supporter (no fellow AIK fan at the table) and to Blåvitt under Hybrid.

## Smaller questions

These matter less, but say what you think.

- **Who can get in?** Anyone · the guild approves each request · invite only · whoever founds the guild chooses.
- **Real clubs or made-up?** Real clubs (Blåvitt, AIK, Malmö FF…) · anything you invent · both. Real crests are trademarked, so real clubs would get their colours, not their badges.
- **One table forever, or seasons?** All-time, where the first big guild leads forever · seasons that reset, with promotion and silverware.
- **When can you switch?** Any time · only in transfer windows, January and summer.
