---
title: Golden goals
date: 2026-10-19
summary: When your crew hits 10, 25, 50, 100 beers and up, every phone at the table goes full screen, and whoever drank the beer that got you there scores the golden goal.
---

Milestones are a moment now, not just a fuller pint.

- **The takeover.** When the crew lands on a rung (10, 25, 50, 100, 250 and on up to 100.000), every phone in the crew lights up: the scoreboard ticks over, the caps fly, and it says who scored. Miss it because you were at home? It's waiting for you when you open the app, for a day.
- **The golden goal.** The beer that hit the number is the golden goal, and its drinker scores it. Whoever tapped it in gets the assist. In a round, it's whichever seat the round happened to land on.
- **The Million.** Every shared beer, everywhere, gets the same treatment at 1.000, 2.500 and on up to 1.000.000.
- **The trophy cabinet.** The crew page lists every rung you've crossed, with who scored it and when. The nights you crossed one get a little trophy in the feed.
- **Your own.** Your 50th, 100th, 250th, 500th and 1.000th beer show under Me. So do two numbers you'll recognise. They never take over the screen: a big night is for the table, not for one person's count.

Taking a beer back doesn't undo a rung. Once it's scored, it's scored.

Rungs your crew reached before today are in the cabinet already. They just don't get a celebration.
