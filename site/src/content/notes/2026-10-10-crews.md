---
title: Parties are crews now
date: 2026-10-10
summary: The group you drink with is called a crew everywhere in the app. Your beers, nights and invite code haven't moved.
---

"Party" sounded like the night out rather than the people you have it with. So the group of friends is a **crew** now, all the way through the app.

- The **Crew** tab replaces Party in the bottom bar.
- Home reads *Crew · 8 members* and counts *beers as a crew*.
- Nothing else changed: every beer, night, streak and invite code is exactly where it was.

A night out is still a night.
