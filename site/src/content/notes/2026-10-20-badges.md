**Combos.** When a night reaches 10 beers, counted across everyone at the table, the whole table is ON FIRE. Then BOOMSHAKALAKA at 25, WORLDIE at 50, RAMPAGE at 100. It's the table's number, not yours: one beer each still counts. A banner drops in on every phone at the table, and the night gets a stamp, there and in the feed. Sit down late? While the night's still open, you get its combos too.

**Badges.** 25 of them, on Me (bottle caps) and on the crew page (beer mats):

- **Showing up:** your debut, ten nights, a night three weeks running, ten weeks running.
- **Giving:** get a round in for the table on ten nights. Ten rounds in one night is still one night.
- **Looking after each other:** drive the night. That means "Driving" on, nothing alcoholic, and somebody else had a beer. It lands two days later, once nothing from the night can change any more. Also your first alcohol-free beer, and a whole week off straight after a week out. Rest counts.
- **The crew:** your first beer, the first friend through the door, five of you, eight at one table, everyone at the same night (once there are four of you or more), and nights two and four weeks running.
- **And a few secrets.** You'll know them when they happen.

Badges come once each and stay: taking a beer back never takes a badge away. Nothing here counts how much you drink in a night. The progress bars only count turning up, nights with a round, driving and the streak you're on.

**Alcohol-free.** Tap someone's name at the table and pick "Alcohol-free one". It counts like any other beer.

Everything that already happened before today is in there too, quietly: no banners for last month.
