---
title: One Million Beers, on your home screen
date: 2026-10-19
summary: Install it like any other app. It opens full screen, straight from your home screen, and it opens even when the pub has no signal.
---

The app can live on your home screen now, next to everything else on your phone. It opens full screen, no browser bars, one tap from the table.

- **Android:** open the app in Chrome and tap **Install** on the banner at the top of Home, or on the card under Me.
- **iPhone:** open it in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Tap **Show me how** in the app and it walks you through it. iPhone keeps home-screen apps apart from Safari, so you sign in once more in there.
- **Signing in on iPhone:** the sign-in email now has a six-digit code as well as the link. The link opens in Safari, so type the code into the app instead.

It also opens with no signal now. You still need a bar or two to log a beer, but that's the next thing to fix.

When there's a new version, a note pops up: **Fresh version on tap**. Tap **Reload**, or just open the app again next time.
