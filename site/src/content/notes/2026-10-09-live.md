The app is out of the workshop: it runs on the web, so anyone in the crew can open it on their phone at [onemillion.fager.tech](https://onemillion.fager.tech).

## Signing in

- **Email and password.** A new account confirms its address with one tap on an emailed link.
- **A sign-in link** instead of a password: type your email, tap the link, you're in.
- **Forgot it?** sends a link that lets you choose a new password.
- A link that has expired or was already used now says so, instead of quietly doing nothing.
- **Continue with Google** is built too. The button appears as soon as it's switched on.
- The emails come dressed in the app's colours, stout black and gold.

## Also new

- **Join a night that's already going.** Someone kicked off without you? Open the night and tap **I'm here — join the table**. Until you do, you can watch the tally but not log.
