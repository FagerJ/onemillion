---
title: Taps work with no signal
date: 2026-10-20
summary: The cellar bar with one bar of signal? Keep tapping. Every + and every round waits on your phone and goes in by itself when the signal's back.
---

The pubs we like have terrible signal. That doesn't matter any more.

- **Keep tapping.** With no signal, `+`, `+ ROUND` and `−` still work. The count goes up straight away, and the taps wait on your phone, "on the slate", until there's signal. Then they go in by themselves, in the order you tapped them, at the time you tapped them.
- **You'll see it.** A little chip at the top says *No signal · 3 waiting*. Tap it to see what's on the slate. When it's all in, you get a "taps off the slate are in".
- **Close the app, it doesn't matter.** Taps are kept even if your phone kills the app. Open it again with no signal and you're straight back on the night.
- **Full time while you were in the cellar?** Taps from before the whistle still go in, up to a day later. Anything tapped after it can't, and the chip tells you: *1 not saved*.
- **Some things still need a bar:** kicking off a night, joining the table, adding someone, the driver toggle and full time. Kick off while you've got signal; after that, tap away.

Combos, milestones and badges still come from the server, so they can land a little later, in the taxi home.
