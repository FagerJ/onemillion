The first version of the app, built for one crew of friends.

## Your crew

- Start a crew and share its **six-character invite code**. It has no I, O, 1 or 0, so it survives being read out in a loud pub.
- Pick a name, initials and a colour. On screen you're a bottle cap.

## A night out

- **Kick off** a night and tick who's at the table. Beers unlock once two of you are there: nobody drinks alone.
- Tap **+** for anyone, **+ ROUND** to pour one for the whole table, and **−** to take one back.
- Driving? Mark yourself and the rounds skip you. You still count as being there.
- Every tap shows up on every phone at the table straight away.
- A night closes itself at 09:00 the next morning, or when someone blows the whistle with **Full time**.

## Home

- **The Million**: every shared beer, everywhere, on one scoreboard.
- Your crew's **pint**, filling towards the next rung of the ladder: 10, 25, 50, 100 … 1.000.000.
- **This week** and your **streak**, counted in weeks with a night out, never days.
- **The table**: who's on top this week, and all time.
- **The nights**: every night the crew has had, newest first, with everyone's count.
