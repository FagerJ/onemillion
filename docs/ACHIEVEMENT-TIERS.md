# Achievement tiers — guild, crew, person

Status: **parked** (2026-10-10) — a draft to curate before the achievement engine
(`G1`–`G3`) is built. Fifty per tier, deliberately over-supplied: cut whatever doesn't
land. It sits beside [ACHIEVEMENTS.md](ACHIEVEMENTS.md), which keeps the combo ladder and
the football and gaming jokes; the names here avoid clashing with it.

**Crew** is the group of friends — it was called a *Party* until 2026-10-10. A night out
is a *night*; "party" isn't used for anything any more.

## How the three tiers work

- **Guild** — collective feats and the really big numbers. Shown on the guild page, and
  every member gets the push.
- **Crew** — firsts, size, streaks and memorable nights. On the crew page, pushed to the crew.
- **Person** — on your own profile. These reward showing up, bringing people, pouring
  rounds, driving and resting — never drinking more in a night. The table earns the glory,
  not the glass.

Pushes celebrate what happened; they never nudge anyone to drink.

Markers: 🔒 secret until unlocked · ⚑ needs something not built yet (named next to it).
Guild entries are counted from beers stamped with the guild, so most work whichever way
[QUESTIONS.md D5](QUESTIONS.md) goes; the ones marked **⚑ D5** assume crews join guilds.

## The badges

Each tier has its own object, so you can tell at a glance whose glory it is:

- **Person → a bottle cap.** People are already caps in the app. Crimped edge, enamel face.
  Rarity by metal: tin, brass, gold, and a holographic foil cap for the rarest.
- **Crew → a beer mat.** The app's cards are already mats. Printed card first, then
  embossed, gold-foiled, and finally framed behind glass.
- **Guild → supporters' club regalia.** Woven patches and scarves, enamel pins, then real
  silverware — cups on plinths for the biggest numbers.

Colours stay in the palette: gold for glory, fire for streaks and live moments, hop green
for rest, driving and alcohol-free.
